Path of Building 2 file in use

recommended · always up to date

In Path of Building 2 click Items and Skills or Passive Tree and Jewels to import your character, then save with Ctrl+S. Choose the build here once – every save in PoB reloads it automatically. The file is only read on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

poe.ninja in use

If your profile is public, poe.ninja lists your character together with its Path of Building code. Open your character on poe.ninja → Builds, copy the address from the browser and paste it here. After that, Refresh loads the latest state with one click.

poe.ninja updates characters on its own schedule, usually every few hours. Changes you made in the last hours may not be there yet.

Path of Building code in use

Works always, also with a private profile: in Path of Building 2 import your character (Import/Export Build → Import from Path of Exile), then Export → Generate → Copy and click here. The clipboard is read once, only when you click.

  1. 1

    Load your character

    Under : in Path of Building 2 import your character and save it (Ctrl+S), then pick that build once – every save in PoB reloads it here. Alternatives: your public poe.ninja profile, or a pasted PoB code.

  2. 2

    See where your build stands

    shows your gear, skills, defences and damage as Path of Building calculates them. Red boxes are things to fix first, e.g. resistances below the cap.

  3. 3

    Check items you find

    In game, hover over an item and press . The overlay answers better, worse or trade-off with the change in damage, effective HP, life and resistances – warnings first. Rings are checked in both slots. Equip in my build keeps the item in your build here until the next import.

    Each press sends exactly one copy command to the game – nothing else.

  4. 4

    Find upgrades for your budget

    Under set a budget per item, how much damage vs. defence counts, and the slots – then Find upgrades. The trade site is searched anonymously and every listing is calculated in your build. Click a listing to open it on the trade site; buying stays with you.

    The trade site allows only a few searches per minute – after a big run, wait a few minutes before the next.

  5. 5

    Follow a guide (optional)

    Under paste the guide's PoB code or a link (pobb.in, poe.ninja …). Each slot becomes a short list of target stats; the item check then also says "Fits your build: 3 of 4", and the page shows what your gear still misses.

  6. 6

    Ask an AI assistant (optional)

    Under you can let Claude, OpenAI Codex, Gemini CLI and others read your build and ask Path of Building about items – off by default.

Good to know

  • All numbers are Path of Building calculations, not measurements in game – conditional effects can differ.
  • Play PoE2 in windowed or windowed fullscreen mode, otherwise the overlay ends up behind the game.
  • No login, no game memory read, nothing injected; the trade site is only searched when you click.

AI connection (MCP)

off

Lets an AI assistant on this computer – Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, VS Code and others that support MCP – read your character and ask Path of Building how an item would change your damage and defences. Completely optional: as long as this is off, the assistant gets nothing.

Local only (no network port). The assistant sees the build loaded here – class, level, gear with mods, skills, keystone passives and the stats PoB calculates. It can change only things in this app – your target build, an item equipped here to try it, or which saved PoB build is used – never your character, nothing in the game, no buying on the trade site.

Set it up in your AI program

Add this server to your AI program once. Claude Desktop: Settings → Developer → Edit config, paste it into claude_desktop_config.json and restart Claude. Other programs (Cursor, VS Code, LM Studio …) use the same mcpServers entry in their MCP settings.

What the assistant can ask

          

            
get_character
Class, ascendancy, level, main skill, every equipped item with mods, jewels, skill gems, keystones.

            
get_stats
Life, energy shield, spirit, evasion, resistances, maximum hits, attributes, DPS – calculated by Path of Building.

            
evaluate_item
Paste an item text: Path of Building shows how much damage and defence it would add or cost in each slot.

            
get_target
Your target build and, slot by slot, which of its stats your gear already has and which are missing.

            
set_target_build
Sets the target build from a PoB code or a link (pobb.in, pastebin, poe.ninja, a page with a PoB code). Shown here right away.

            
get_skill_details
All numbers of one skill – hit, crit, speed, projectiles, area – and its supports.

            
get_defence_details
Every defence and recovery number: resistances and maximum hit per element, evasion, deflection, regeneration …

            
get_last_item_check
The item you last checked in game with the hotkey – just ask "is this item good?".

            
what_if
"What would +30% chaos resistance or +1 projectile level give me?" – PoB calculates it without changing your build.

            
price_item
poe.ninja price of currency, runes, omens or a unique, with the check of all trade pairs.

            
equip_item
Equips an item in this app's copy of your build, to try several upgrades one after another. "Reset" undoes it.

            
list_builds / use_build
Your builds saved in Path of Building 2, and switching to another one (e.g. a bossing setup).

            
search_upgrades
The upgrade search: trade listings within a budget, calculated in your build, with links. Up to 3 slots per question.

          

          
Try: "Which of my items should I upgrade first?", "Is this ring better than mine?" with the item text, or "Use this guide as my target and tell me what I'm missing" with a pobb.in link.
